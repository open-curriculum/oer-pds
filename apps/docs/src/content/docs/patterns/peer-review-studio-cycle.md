---
title: Peer Review Studio Cycle
description: An iterative pattern of draft, critique, revision, and final submission structured around peer review.
---

**Pattern ID:** `peer-review-studio-cycle`

**Best for:** Advanced projects, iterative creative production, collaborative critique, portfolio capstones.

## Sequence

### 1. Draft
Produce an initial version of the artifact.

### 2. Peer Review
Exchange work and give structured written feedback.

### 3. Revision
Revise based on peer feedback.

### 4. Final Submission
Submit final work with a revision statement.

## Assessment Evidence
- Draft
- Peer feedback given
- Revised artifact
- Revision statement
- Final submission
