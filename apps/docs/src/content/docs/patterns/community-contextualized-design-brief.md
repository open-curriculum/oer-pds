title: Community-Contextualized Design Brief
description: A community-engaged pattern where learners design for or with a real community partner.
---

**Pattern ID:** `community-contextualized-design-brief`

**Best for:** Community-engaged design, local media production, participatory projects, service learning, open model artifacts.

## Sequence

### 1. Community Context
Research and document the community context, needs, and values.

### 2. Design Brief
Develop a design brief in response to the community context.

### 3. Prototype or Draft
Produce a prototype or draft artifact.

### 4. Community Feedback
Share prototype with community stakeholders for feedback.

### 5. Revision
Revise based on community feedback.

### 6. Ethical Reflection
Reflect on the ethics and positionality of the design work.

## Assessment Evidence
- Community context statement
- Design brief
- Prototype
- Stakeholder feedback documentation
- Revised artifact
- Ethical reflection
