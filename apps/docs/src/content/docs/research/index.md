The OIS vocabulary is grounded in established instructional design research and frameworks.

## Key Frameworks

### Universal Design for Learning (UDL)
OIS `accessibility.*` tokens and the `accessibility-first` theme implement core UDL principles: multiple means of representation, multiple means of action and expression, and multiple means of engagement.

**Reference:** CAST (2018). Universal Design for Learning Guidelines version 2.2. [http://udlguidelines.cast.org](http://udlguidelines.cast.org)

### Bloom's Taxonomy
The OIS `assessment.evidence` token values correspond to different levels of Bloom's cognitive taxonomy, from foundational knowledge retrieval (`quiz`) to synthesis and creation (`artifact-plus-reflection`).

**Reference:** Bloom, B.S. (1956). Taxonomy of Educational Objectives. David McKay Company.

### Constructivism and Situated Learning
The `constructivist-studio` and `inquiry-based` themes implement constructivist principles — knowledge is built through active meaning-making, not received. The studio critique cycle implements Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development and Lave & Wenger's situated learning.

**References:**
- Vygotsky, L.S. (1978). Mind in Society. Harvard University Press.
- Lave, J. & Wenger, E. (1991). Situated Learning. Cambridge University Press.

### Mastery Learning
The `mastery-learning` theme and `mastery-checkpoint-pathway` pattern implement Bloom's mastery learning model: students must demonstrate proficiency before advancing, with multiple chances to revise.

**Reference:** Bloom, B.S. (1968). Learning for mastery. Evaluation Comment, 1(2).

### Critical Pedagogy
The `critical-media-analysis` and `community-engaged` themes draw on critical pedagogy's emphasis on power, voice, reflection, and social transformation.

**Reference:** Freire, P. (1970). Pedagogy of the Oppressed. Herder and Herder.

### OERSchema and Linked Data
OIS token identifiers are designed to extend OERSchema, making OIS metadata compatible with existing linked open data ecosystems.

**Reference:** [https://oerschema.org](https://oerschema.org)
